## Voice Command Examples:

You can now search for products using natural language:

- **"find apples"** → Directs to Produce section
- **"where is milk"** → Directs to Dairy section
- **"locate shampoo"** → Directs to Pharmacy
- **"find dog food"** → Directs to Pet Care aisle
- **"where are the chips"** → Directs to Aisle 3
- **"find frozen pizza"** → Directs to Aisle 5
- **"locate bread"** → Directs to Bakery
- **"where is toilet paper"** → Directs to Household aisle

## Brand-Specific Searches:
Many products include brand name aliases:
- **"find cheerios"** → Finds Cereal in Aisle 1
- **"where is coke"** → Finds Soda in Aisle 4
- **"find tide"** → Finds Laundry Detergent in Household
- **"locate pampers"** → Finds Diapers in Baby Care
